## Superseded decisions and what replaced them
The register keeps every row; this table records only what was replaced. Rationale and history: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 10.
| Earlier position | Replaced by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Tesseract is the only OCR engine (original plan, M4) | D-022 RapidOCR primary, Tesseract as extra evidence and fallback; D-023 cascade and agreement rules | On real photographs Tesseract read no batch line (27 of 27 photos `Unable to verify`); the investigation found the engine, not preprocessing, dominated (`docs/OCR_INVESTIGATION.md`) |
| D-021 no user-editable fields | D-024 manual batch entry with provenance (`BatchSource`) | Needed for the common "no batch read" case without weakening matching |
| A clean OCR negative is shown as `No alert found` once the batch is read consistently | D-026 no clean negative until the user confirms the printed number | Two engines agreed on a wrong look-alike character; agreement is not independence |
| Blister-strip 3D hero (D-029) | D-030 five-scene story | Redesign after the owner's review; same offline, frontend-only architecture |
| Result shown as native status boxes and text lines | D-031 result presentation (cards, banner, "Confirmed by you") | Readability; the engine's wording is unchanged apart from the one approved line |
| Static chips listing the OCR's batch readings | D-032 clickable readings that fill the batch box | Removes retyping; a click never confirms or submits |

**Not superseded:** D-014. It stays OPEN pending further evaluation planning. The original 60-image proposal and the evidence-category revision are both unapproved and unexecuted; neither replaces the other until the owner decides (see the D-014 row).

Open architectural observations for the independent audit are recorded in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 12 (OA-1 to OA-13). They are not decisions.

## Approval protocol for OPEN items
Record owner, date, decision, rationale, affected docs and tests. Agents must not turn an OPEN item into an approved one.
